# Regulatory Framework: Indian E-Waste Management Rules & Recycler Verification

## 1. Official Regulatory Framework

This project's guidance engine is grounded in official notifications from the **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)**, Government of India:

1. **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022:**
   - **Notification:** G.S.R. 801(E), published in the Gazette of India on **November 2, 2022**.
   - **Enforcement Date:** Came into force on **April 1, 2023** (superseding the 2016 rules).
   - **Key Provisions:**
     - **Chapter II & Schedule I:** Expanded categories of Electrical and Electronic Equipment (EEE) covered, including IT/telecom equipment, consumer electronics, and large/small appliances.
     - **Chapter III & Schedule III:** Establishes mandatory **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)** quotas for producers, requiring certified recycling of historical and current sales volumes.
     - **Chapter IV:** Strict operational requirements for registered dismantlers and recyclers, prohibiting informal acid bath extraction and open incineration.
     - **Rule 14 & Rule 21:** Environmental compensation penalties for improper disposal, dumping in municipal landfills, or dealing with unauthorized scrap dealers.

2. **Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022:**
   - **Notification:** G.S.R. 664(E), published on **August 24, 2022**.
   - **Scope:** Applies to Portable batteries, Electric Vehicle (EV) batteries, Automotive batteries, and Industrial batteries.
   - **Mandate:** Establishes a 100% recovery and collection mechanism via Buy-back/Deposit-Refund systems.

## 2. Authoritative Regulatory Bodies & Verification Guidelines

When conducting local field verifications or updating the recycler directory for Bengaluru:

### Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- **Role:** National monitoring authority, manages the centralized online EPR Portal for E-Waste.
- **Search phrase for students:** `"CPCB List of Registered E-Waste Recyclers and Dismantlers"`
- **Official portal:** `cpcb.nic.in` / `eprewastecpcb.in` *(VERIFY: Check latest monthly circular updates)*.

### Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)
- **Jurisdiction:** Bengaluru Urban and Rural districts.
- **Role:** Issues Consent for Establishment (CFE) and Consent for Operation (CFO) to authorized dismantlers in industrial areas (e.g., Peenya, Electronic City, Whitefield, Bommasandra).
- **Search phrase for students:** `"KSPCB authorized e-waste recyclers Bengaluru list pdf"`
- **Official portal:** `kspcb.karnataka.gov.in` *(VERIFY: Check validity period of recycler authorization)*.

## 3. Class-Specific Safe Disposal Protocols

| Class | Primary Hazard | Regulatory Channel | Key Safety Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`battery`** | Corrosive electrolyte & Lithium fire hazard | Battery Waste Rules 2022 (EPR buyback) | Tape terminals with non-conductive electrical tape |
| **`cfl_bulb`** | Mercury (Hg) vapor | E-Waste Rules 2022 (Lighting equipment) | Do NOT break; evacuate & ventilate 15 min if broken |
| **`monitor_crt`** | Lead glass & Vacuum implosion | E-Waste Rules 2022 (Hazardous CRT recycling) | Handle by front frame; never crack neck glass |
| **`mobile_phone`** | Lithium battery & precious metals | OEM Take-Back / Authorized Dismantler | Perform data factory reset before handover |
| **`pcb`** | Lead solder & Brominated Flame Retardants | Authorized Hydrometallurgical Recyclers | Formal processing only; no open acid-leaching |
| **`keyboard`** | Recyclable plastics & circuit traces | E-Waste dry drop box / EPR drive | Segregate cables; keep dry |
| **`other`** | Mixed municipal waste | BBMP Dry Waste Collection Centers (DWCC) | Inspect for embedded batteries or electrical cords |
